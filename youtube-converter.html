YouTube → MP3 / MP4

Paste a YouTube link to preview the video, or convert a video file on your device to MP3 or MP4.

Open on YouTube to watch or use YouTube’s built-in controls. Direct MP3/MP4 download from YouTube isn’t available in the browser (platform rules).

Open on YouTube

Browsers can’t pull audio/video files straight from YouTube. This tool previews the link via public oEmbed data. For files you own, use the Local file tab to convert on your device.

Drop a video/audio file here or click to browse

Local conversion uses your browser only — files stay on your device. MP3 extraction works best from video files the browser can decode (e.g. MP4/WebM). Complex codecs may not be supported without a desktop app.
